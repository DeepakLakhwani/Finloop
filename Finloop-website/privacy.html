Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 26, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how FinLoop collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Financial Information

FinLoop may store:

  • Transactions (expenses, income, and transfers)
  • Categories (custom colors and icons)
  • Account names and settings
  • Account balances
  • Notes and scratchpad records
  • Budget records and spending thresholds

This information is stored solely for app functionality and is managed under your control.

Device Information

We may collect diagnostic data for improving app stability and performance, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version and build number
  • Crash logs and system diagnostics

Authentication Information

If enabled by the user:

  • Biometric authentication settings (Fingerprint / Face ID configuration)
  • 4-digit Passcode settings

These settings are securely handled using device-level security systems. FinLoop does NOT access, store, or transmit your raw fingerprint or Face ID data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to enable and improve the core financial tracking features, including:

  • Providing finance tracking and transaction calculations
  • Generating category distribution and budget charts
  • Syncing scratchpad notes and records
  • Improving app performance and fixing system crashes
  • Securing user accounts and validating session locks
  • Enabling data export/import (Excel, Backup, PDF) functionality

3. Data Storage

Local Storage

Most of your financial information is stored locally on the user's device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data without backing it up, this data may be permanently lost.

Cloud Synchronization

Certain optional features such as scratchpad notes, cloud backups, and account sync services use secure cloud infrastructure provided by Firebase Firestore.

4. Data Sharing

FinLoop does NOT sell user data. We do not share personal financial information with third parties except in these specific instances:

  • When required by law or regulatory authorities
  • When necessary for core app functionality (e.g., executing secure cloud sync)
  • With secure, industry-standard infrastructure providers (e.g., Firebase, Google Mobile Ads)

5. Data Security

We implement modern security measures to protect your database, including:

  • Secure local storage encryption mechanisms
  • Encrypted communication channels for cloud sync (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Device-level biometric validation overlays
  • Smart auto-lock security triggers on session timeout

However, please be aware that no security system can guarantee 100% absolute protection against all vulnerabilities.

6. Excel Import & Export

Users have full power to export financial data to formatted Excel (.xlsx) files or compile them into PDF records. Since these files are saved to external device storage, users are solely responsible for protecting and securing these exported files when shared outside the app.

7. Notifications

FinLoop may send local notifications, such as daily expense entry reminders or budget threshold alerts. You can completely customize or disable notifications at any time through the in-app settings or your device OS settings.

8. Biometric Authentication

Biometric authentication (Fingerprint / Face ID) is entirely optional. Biometric checks are handled natively by your device's operating system. FinLoop only receives a success/fail confirmation and never has access to your biometric prints.

9. Children's Privacy

FinLoop is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from children under 13.

10. User Rights

You have full authority over your data. At any time, you can:

  • Delete all in-app transaction data
  • Disable cloud backup and synchronization
  • Remove passcode locks or change security settings
  • Export your entire transaction ledger to Excel/PDF
  • Uninstall the application to stop all local data processing

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users are encouraged to review this page regularly for any updates. Continued use of FinLoop constitutes acceptance of any policy revisions.

12. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or data security in FinLoop, feel free to reach out to us:
